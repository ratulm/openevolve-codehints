(a) The program picks 24 taps for a causal FIR filter. The filter has to denoise piecewise-linear drift signals that contain abrupt jumps and are corrupted by coloured (AR(1), coefficient 0.7) plus white noise. The output must track the jumps quickly, not just smooth the signal.

(b) combined_score is the reference cost divided by the filter's cost over the 64 hidden signals:

combined_score = 1.452 / (E_track + 8·E_lag + 0.1·E_rough)

E_track is the MSE to the clean signal, E_lag is the jump-normalised squared error over the 8 samples after each jump, and E_rough is the mean squared output difference. Any invalid output scores 0.
